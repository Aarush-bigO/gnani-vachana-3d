- **Voice Biometrics:** Gnani armour365™ (Deepfake acoustic liveness verification and scam gateway trunk neutralization).
- **3D Digital Human:** Three.js WebGL (60 FPS phoneme-viseme lip-sync, dynamic hair & bone physics, and physical Indic gestures like *Namaste* and *Wave*).
- **Database Engine:** Pure Python in-memory inverted token hash index across **30,433 structured records** with **< 1.5ms** retrieval latency across 9 domains.
