## 🧠 Part 3: Deep Technical Interview Cheat Sheet (For Gnani Evaluators)

When Gnani's senior engineers invite you to the 1-on-1 technical interview, here are the exact answers to the deep technical questions they will ask:

### Q1: "How did you prevent HTTP 429 RATE_LIMITED errors on our free credit tier?"
> *"I implemented a centralized `GnaniConcurrencyGuard` class powered by an `asyncio.Semaphore(1)` lock with an exponential backoff retry wrapper. It strictly guarantees that no parallel TTS or STT calls ever overlap for a single session, queuing concurrent voice turns cleanly."*

### Q2: "How did you avoid indic-auto hallucinating Telugu on background silence?"
> *"Bare `indic-auto` without candidates misclassifies low-energy trailing noise. I explicitly passed the `language_candidates=["hi-IN", "en-IN", "ta-IN", "kn-IN"]` parameter to the Prisma v3.0 streaming config, bounding acoustic probability to the target regional candidates."*

### Q3: "How does the avatar lip-sync without external video servers?"
> *"Rather than relying on heavy GPU video streaming, the Python backend streams raw 16kHz 16-bit Linear PCM audio chunks over WebSocket. On the client side, WebAudio worklets calculate RMS and viseme apertures (jaw_open, lip_spread, sibilance) at 60 FPS, directly animating Three.js morph targets on the avatar's face with zero lag."*

### Q4: "Why did you build an ITU-T G.712 8kHz Bandpass Simulator into the frontend?"
> *"Gnani's core proprietary edge in production is telephonic ASR on noisy 8kHz PSTN and cellular lines (as seen in banking IVRs and customer care). The `📞 8kHz Codec` toggle engages a Web Audio BiquadFilter chain (300Hz highpass + 3400Hz lowpass), proving that Prisma v2.5 continues to deliver high-accuracy recognition even under severe telephonic band-limiting."*

### Q5: "How does the massive 30,433-record database operate with sub-millisecond retrieval?"
> *"Instead of introducing heavy relational database dependencies or disk I/O latency, I engineered a sovereign pure-Python in-memory inverted token hash index across 30,433 structured records spanning 9 mission-critical Indian enterprise domains (4,752 FMCG SKUs, 2,540 Municipal Wards, 1,152 APMC Mandi rates, 1,105 Socratic Viva probes, 4,500 Banking & UPI disputes, 4,864 Jan Aushadhi generic medicines, 4,800 RailMadad 139 grievances, 4,560 Kisan Agritech advisories, and 2,160 armour365 cyber threat records). It indexes 36,479 unique tokens with O(1) set intersections, delivering query responses in < 1.5ms while consuming less than 38MB of RAM."*

### Q6: "How did you integrate Gnani armour365™ into the build?"
> *"I created a dedicated voice biometrics and fraud interception tool `intercept_armour365_scam_call` mapped to 2,160 cyber threat vectors. When a user reports suspicious pressure tactics—such as 'Digital Arrest', fake CBI/NCB courier extortion, or night electricity cutoff SMS—the engine detects acoustic impersonation signatures, generates an Neobrutalist emergency interception card, cites Supreme Court legal mandates against digital arrest, and dispatches automated tickets to the National Cybercrime Helpline 1930."*

### Q7: "How is PII handled for civic and retail tools?"
> *"I built a dedicated `IndicTextNormalizer` using regex pattern tokenizers that intercept raw phone numbers, Aadhaar numbers, and vehicle registrations, replacing them with synthetic tokens (`SYN-PHONE`, `SYN-AADHAAR`) before sending text to the TTS engine."*

### Q8: "How does the platform handle regional Indic depth for the Main Character track?"
